1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, information collected automatically when you use our website, and information we receive from third parties.

Information you provide to us

  • Contact details — name, phone number, email address, mailing address, and company name (for shippers and brokers).
  • Driver application data — full name, phone, email, home-base state, CDL class, years of experience, preferred equipment type, endorsements (hazmat, TWIC), MVR status, and any additional information you choose to share in the application.
  • Freight and quote details — lane origin/destination, weight, equipment needs, and delivery dates submitted through our quote requests.
  • Communications — anything you send us by email, phone, or through website forms.

Information collected automatically

  • IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system.
  • Pages visited, time spent on the site, and referring/exit pages.
  • Approximate location derived from IP address.

2. How We Collect Information

  • Directly from you — when you fill out the driver application, request a quote, or contact dispatch or recruiting.
  • Automatically — through standard web technologies such as cookies and server logs as you browse our site.
  • From third parties — such as background-check and MVR providers (for driver applicants, and only with your authorization), load boards, and referral partners.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide freight quotes, dispatch loads, and manage shipments for reefer and RGN 53' lanes.
  • Evaluate and process driver applications, including scheduling interviews and conducting required background, MVR, and drug-screening checks.
  • Communicate with you about your load, application, or inquiry.
  • Maintain required DOT, FMCSA, and insurance records.
  • Improve our website, services, and dispatch operations.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • Comply with legal obligations, including transportation and employment recordkeeping requirements.

4. Driver Applicants

If you submit a driver application through our website, the information you provide (name, phone, email, home state, CDL class, experience, equipment preference, endorsements, and any notes) is used solely to evaluate your application and to allow a TFS recruiter to contact you about driving opportunities. We do not sell driver applicant data. Where required — such as for a motor vehicle record (MVR) check, background check, or drug screening — we will obtain your authorization before requesting that information from a third-party provider.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who perform functions on our behalf, such as background-check firms, payroll and settlement processors, ELD/telematics providers, and website hosting.
  • Shippers, brokers, and receiving facilities as needed to coordinate pickup and delivery of a load.
  • Government and regulatory agencies (e.g., FMCSA, DOT) where required by law or applicable transportation regulation.
  • Professional advisors such as legal, insurance, and accounting partners.
  • A successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of company assets.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, understand how visitors use the site, and support basic site functionality. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent; some parts of the site may not function properly without them.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, including satisfying legal, accounting, insurance, or reporting requirements. FMCSA and DOT regulations require certain driver and safety records to be retained for specific periods (typically three years or more) even after employment or contracting ends.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to our legal and recordkeeping obligations.
  • Opt out of non-essential marketing communications by using the unsubscribe link or contacting us directly.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details at the bottom of this page. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are directed at businesses and individuals aged 18 and older (in line with CDL eligibility requirements). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be posted here.
